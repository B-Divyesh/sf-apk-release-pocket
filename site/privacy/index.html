Plain-language policy

Privacy

Effective August 28, 2026.

The CLI

APK Release Pocket processes APKs entirely on your computer. It does not upload APKs, fingerprints, release metadata, or credentials, and contains no telemetry.

The website

The site does not use analytics, advertising, third-party fonts, or tracking cookies. Your host may retain standard short-lived access logs for security and delivery.

Paid unlock

If you buy Team lantern, Sociobot/Dodo handles checkout as merchant of record. This site stores the license token at sb_license:apk-release-pocket, its latest verification result, and team audit notes in your browser’s local storage. Verification sends the token to api.sociobot.in at most once daily. Remove site data in your browser to delete it.

Contact

Open an issue in the public repository for privacy questions. Do not post a license token or private APK.
